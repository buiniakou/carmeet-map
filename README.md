# Car Meet Spots

My GTA V car meet spots on the satellite map.

- Click the map to drop a spot: name it and tag it Car meet, Cruise route and/or Photo spot.
- Spots save in your browser automatically.
- The shared spots are hardcoded in `BUILT_IN` in `index.html`, so every visitor sees them. Edits and deletes you make stay in your browser.
- **Export** downloads `spots.json`; paste its entries into `BUILT_IN` to share new spots.
- The map is drawn with MapLibre from zoom tiles in `tiles/` (cut from the 8192px satellite image).
