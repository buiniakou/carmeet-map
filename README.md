# Car Meet Spots

My GTA V car meet spots on the satellite map.

- Click the map to drop a spot: name it and pick Car meet, Cruise route or Photo spot.
- Spots save in your browser automatically.
- **Export** downloads `spots.json`. Commit it to this repo and any new browser/device loads your spots on first visit.
- The map is drawn with MapLibre from zoom tiles in `tiles/` (cut from the 8192px satellite image).
